## Roles

### Staff Roles

1. **Owner** — Full server and project authority.
2. **Administrator** — Server administration and configuration.
3. **Community Manager** — Community operations, events, and announcements.
4. **Game Master** — In-game events, disputes, and player assistance.
5. **Moderator** — Rules enforcement, reports, and chat moderation.
6. **Support Team** — Account, technical, and ticket support.
7. **Event Host** — Permission to organize and manage community events.

### Community Roles

- **Verified Player** — Completed onboarding and accepted the rules.
- **Alliance Leader** — Leader of a recognized alliance.
- **Alliance Officer** — Officer of a recognized alliance.
- **Content Creator** — Approved creator sharing Atlantic-Wars content.
- **Veteran** — Experienced community member.
- **New Player** — Optional onboarding role for beginners.

### Notification Roles

- **Game News** — Version updates and official announcements.
- **Maintenance Alerts** — Downtime and service notifications.
- **Event Alerts** — Tournament and event notifications.
- **Patch Notes** — Detailed change notifications.

### Language Roles

- **Portuguese**
- **English**
- **Spanish**

## Permission Model

- `@everyone` can see `#welcome`, `#rules`, `#announcements`, and `#how-to-play`.
- Only staff can publish in `#announcements`, `#server-status`, and `#leaderboards`.
- Only verified players can participate in gameplay and community discussions.
- Alliance channels are visible to verified players; private alliance rooms are created separately by staff approval.
- Support channels must not expose private account or payment information publicly.
- Staff channels are visible only to the relevant staff roles.
- Moderators can timeout, remove messages, and manage threads, but cannot manage server ownership or administrator roles.
- No staff role should receive more permissions than required for its duties.

## Moderation Workflow

1. A member reports content or a moderator identifies a violation.
2. The moderator records the relevant message, member, rule, action, and timestamp in `#mod-log`.
3. Minor issues receive a reminder or warning.
4. Repeated or serious violations may receive a timeout, kick, or ban according to severity.
5. Account, payment, and security matters move to a private ticket.
6. Appeals are reviewed by a staff member who was not the original decision-maker whenever possible.
